Estructuras en inglés

Las estructuras nos ayudan a formar oraciones correctamente. El orden de las palabras es importante para que una oración tenga sentido.

Simple Tenses

FormGrammar structure
AffirmativeSubject + verb + complement
NegativeSubject + auxiliary + do + not + verb + complement
InterrogativeAuxiliary + subject + verb + complement + ?

Continuous Tenses

FormGrammar structure
AffirmativeSubject + auxiliary be + verb in gerund + complement
NegativeSubject + auxiliary be + not + verb in gerund + complement
InterrogativeAuxiliary be + subject + verb in gerund + complement + ?

Perfect Tenses

FormGrammar structure
AffirmativeSubject + auxiliary have + verb in past participle + complement
NegativeSubject + auxiliary have + not + verb in past participle + complement
InterrogativeAuxiliary have + subject + verb in past participle + complement + ?

Idiomatic Future (be going to)

FormGrammar structure
AffirmativeSubject + verb to be + going to + verb in infinitive + complement
NegativeSubject + verb to be + not + going to + verb in infinitive + complement
InterrogativeVerb to be + subject + going to + verb in infinitive + complement + ?

Modal Verb Structures

FormGrammar structure
AffirmativeSubject + modal verb + verb base form + complement
NegativeSubject + modal verb + not + verb base form + complement
InterrogativeModal verb + subject + verb base form + complement + ?

Passive Voice

FormGrammar structure
Active sentenceSubject + verb + object
Passive sentenceObject + verb to be + verb in past participle + by + subject

Conditional Sentences

ConditionalConditionResult
0If + simple presentSimple present
1If + simple presentSimple future
2If + simple pastWould + verb
3If + past perfectWould have + past participle

Oraciones negativas

Para negar con el verbo to be, agregamos not.


Ejemplo:
I am not tired.


Significado:
No estoy cansado/a.
